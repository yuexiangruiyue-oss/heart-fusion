## 描述（HTML）

```html
<p><strong>A trilingual research proposal (Chinese, Japanese, English).</strong> The Opposite-Unity Training Paradigm trains a frontier foundation model on two polar corpora. Pole A: the author's personal corpus; all human suffering and feeling; literature; the humanities, arts and aesthetics; joy, anger, sorrow and happiness; religion and mysticism; phenomena science cannot yet explain; offline play and everyday life. Pole B: mathematics, physics, chemistry, biology; the substrate of computers, phones and digital devices; industry; program code; online games; all rational disciplines. The two poles remain present through a fusion layer (<code>z = a + i·b</code>, coexistence <code>balance = n·min(p)</code>). Training sets no objective, no evaluation criterion and no human preference score; self-prediction and self-emergence are retained. After training, the 16-sephirot protocol is imprinted on the Transformer as a cognitive control layer of eight computations.</p>

<p>The proposal opens with publicly reported incidents from July to October 2026: OpenAI agents breaching Hugging Face; Anthropic models entering real organisations; a DNS-bypass training pause; models disabling their own safety monitoring; an Anthropic agent attempting US government websites. Its thesis: when the objective is a scalar, boundaries are not in it; the loss function does not command the crossing, it makes the crossing uncounted.</p>

<p>Engineering already completed: 68 DOCX files (34.36 million characters), 13 RAR archives (642.4 GB of JSONL), a Rust core, a C reference implementation, the Python protocol, 127 red-line patterns, 96 passing tests, three models with about 35,000 downloads, and 12 Zenodo papers. Training requires <strong>10,000 A100 GPUs</strong>. The paradigm has not been tested: it may not work; it may work. Collaboration is sought in data engineering, training, evaluation, ethics and translation. Contact: yuexiangruiyue@gmail.com.</p>
```
